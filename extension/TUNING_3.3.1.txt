YURIKA Audio 3.3.1 - Adaptive Safety Stability

- Pressure attack unchanged: -0.25 dB steps after sustained limiter/peak pressure.
- Adaptive trim release no longer occurs during active program audio.
- Release is allowed only after 8 consecutive quiet safety ticks (peak < 0.08 OR RMS < -42 dBFS).
- Quiet release step: +0.10 dB with 0.45 s ramp.
- New diagnostics: adaptiveTrimHoldReason, adaptiveTrimQuietStreak, adaptiveTrimLastPressureAtMs, adaptiveTrimReleaseCount.
- Self-DAP coefficients, HRTF, Spatial and Virtual Amp coefficients unchanged.
