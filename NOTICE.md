## Headphone correction data

`extension/headphone-profiles.js` contains a small starter registry whose metadata identifies AutoEq/oratory1990-derived measurement families for selected headphone models. The source URL recorded by the project is:

- https://github.com/jaakkopasanen/AutoEq

Users redistributing or expanding measurement-derived presets should independently verify the applicable licenses and permissions for the underlying measurements/data.
